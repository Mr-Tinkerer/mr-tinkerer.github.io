## Overview

This page documents terms used across the SearXNG bucket.

## SearXNG

A free, self-hostable metasearch engine that aggregates results from other search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, etc.) into one result set, without tracking the person searching or forwarding queries to those engines under their own identity.

It provides a private web-search backend for local models, instead of depending on a third-party search API with its own cost, rate limits, and data-sharing terms. SearXNG fetches live results when a local model needs current information rather than just training data.

See [SearXNG (official site)](https://docs.searxng.org/).
